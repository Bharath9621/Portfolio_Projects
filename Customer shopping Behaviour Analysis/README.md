# 🛒 Consumer Shopping Behavior Analytics

An end-to-end analytics project that explores customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and sales trends using SQL, Python, and Power BI. The project provides business insights that help organizations improve marketing strategies, customer segmentation, and revenue growth.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving customer retention and maximizing sales performance.

This project analyzes shopping transactions to identify customer trends, product demand, and purchasing patterns through interactive dashboards.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

This project answers questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which categories perform best?
- What purchasing trends exist over time?
- How do customer demographics influence buying behavior?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?

---

# 🚀 Business Problems Solved

### Customer Segmentation

Grouped customers based on purchasing behavior.

### Sales Performance

Tracked sales growth across products and categories.

### Product Analytics

Identified top-performing and underperforming products.

### Revenue Optimization

Analyzed purchasing trends to support marketing decisions.

### Executive Reporting

Built dashboards providing actionable sales insights.
